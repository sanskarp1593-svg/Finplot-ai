# Mandatory Invoice Fields Under GST

## Tax Invoice Requirements
Every registered business must issue a tax invoice for supply of goods or services. A valid GST tax invoice must contain:

1. **Supplier details**: Name, address, and GSTIN of the supplier
2. **Invoice number**: A unique, sequential number for the financial year (max 16 characters, can include /, -)
3. **Date of issue**
4. **Recipient details**: Name, address, and GSTIN/UIN (if registered); name and address with state + state code (if unregistered, value > ₹50,000)
5. **HSN/SAC code**: Harmonized System of Nomenclature code for goods, or Service Accounting Code for services
6. **Description of goods/services**
7. **Quantity and unit** (for goods)
8. **Total value before tax**
9. **Taxable value** (after discounts)
10. **Tax rate and amount**: CGST, SGST/UTGST (intra-state) or IGST (inter-state), and cess if applicable
11. **Total invoice value** (in figures and words)
12. **Place of supply** (with state name and code)
13. **Reverse charge**: Whether tax is payable on reverse charge basis
14. **Signature or digital signature** of the supplier or authorised representative

## Bill of Supply
Composition dealers and those supplying exempt goods issue a Bill of Supply instead of a Tax Invoice. It does not include tax breakup.

## Revised Invoice
If the original invoice had errors, a revised or supplementary invoice (credit/debit note) must be issued within September of the next financial year or before filing of the annual return, whichever is earlier.

## Penalties for Non-Compliance
- Failure to issue an invoice: penalty of ₹10,000 or the tax amount, whichever is higher
- Issuing incorrect invoice: similar penalty and potential prosecution for large amounts
