# GST Basics for Small Businesses

## What is GST?
Goods and Services Tax (GST) is an indirect tax levied on the supply of goods and services in India. It replaced multiple earlier taxes like VAT, service tax, and excise duty.

## GST Registration Threshold
- **Goods**: Businesses with annual turnover above ₹40 lakh (₹20 lakh for special category states) must register for GST.
- **Services**: The threshold is ₹20 lakh (₹10 lakh for special category states).
- Voluntary registration is allowed below these limits.

## GST Rates
Common GST rate slabs: 0%, 5%, 12%, 18%, and 28%.
- Essential goods (unbranded rice, wheat, milk): 0%
- Packaged food items: 5% or 12%
- Most services: 18%
- Luxury and sin goods (tobacco, aerated drinks): 28% + cess

## Composition Scheme
Small businesses with turnover up to ₹1.5 crore can opt for the Composition Scheme:
- Pay tax at a fixed rate (1% for manufacturers, 5% for restaurants, 6% for other services)
- File quarterly returns instead of monthly
- Cannot collect tax from customers or claim input tax credit
- Cannot do inter-state supply

## Filing Frequency
- Regular taxpayers: Monthly GSTR-1 (outward supplies) and GSTR-3B (summary return)
- Composition taxpayers: Quarterly CMP-08 and annual GSTR-4
- QRMP scheme: Quarterly filing with monthly payment for turnover up to ₹5 crore
